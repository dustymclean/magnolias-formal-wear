Buy one, get one half off ✨

Buy one children’s ticket, get one adult ticket ½ price. Children’s tickets start at $45 per event. For events with higher food costs, the children’s ticket may be $50 or $55; the adult ticket is always half the children’s ticket price when purchased together.

Typical family pair: $45 child + $22.50 adult = $67.50 total. At food-inclusive events, a $50 child + $25 adult = $75, or a $55 child + $27.50 adult = $82.50.

Applies to one adult ticket with the purchase of one children’s ticket. Prices are proposed until each event is scheduled and registration opens; the final ticket price and food inclusions will be announced for each event.
