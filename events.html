THE MAGNOLIAS EXPERIENCE / WINTER WONDERLAND

A Christmas storybook, written in velvet and starlight.

Imagine opening a beautifully wrapped gift and stepping straight inside. That is the feeling behind Magnolias Christmas Pageant: an intimate holiday celebration dressed in snowy ivory, antique champagne gold, cranberry velvet, twinkling lights, and Magnolia-pink ribbon. Think frosted gingerbread cottages, evergreen garlands, sparkling crowns, and the unmistakable joy of being part of a Christmas memory.

Contestants are invited to bring their most confident selves to a warm, festive stage. Whether their personal style is classic holiday elegance, soft winter-princess glamour, or something delightfully playful, the spotlight belongs to them.

✧ Style & stage inspiration
  • Color story: cranberry, ivory, pine green, champagne gold, rose pink and silver sparkle
  • Wardrobe inspiration: holiday formalwear, rich velvet, bows, winter whites, glittering accessories or a favorite festive dress; confirm individual category rules before purchasing an outfit
  • Stage mood: a sparkling holiday entrance, poised presentation and a celebratory crowning atmosphere
✧ Event details & registration

Confirmed: Sunday, December 13, 2026 at Magnolias Formal Wear, 100 Railroad Street, Water Valley, Mississippi. Registration is 12:00–1:30 PM and the pageant begins at 2:00 PM. The advertised entry deadline is December 5, 2026.

Advertised fees: $50 beauty entry; optional $20 side awards, $40 outfit of choice, $45 overall supreme titles, $10 photogenic, $5 per additional photo (up to 3 extra), and $5 judges’ sheets. The $100 package includes beauty, side awards and outfit of choice. Door admission is $10 for ages 2 and older; late entries are advertised at an additional $25, subject to acceptance. All entry fees are due when the application is turned in.
