Ask Magnolias ↗

Babies, toddlers & little girls

Ask about age-appropriate formal styles for babies, toddlers, flower girls, fairy princess moments and children’s pageant looks. Child comfort and guardian approval always come first.

Juniors & tweens

For school celebrations, junior pageants, performances, birthdays, family portraits and dress-up parties, share the occasion, general measurements and desired style.

Teens & young adults

Prom, HOCO, homecoming, school dances, pageants, graduation celebrations and formal events can call for very different silhouettes and coverage preferences.

Adults & family occasions

Adult formalwear can include weddings, elegant parties, galas, church events and memorial services. Inclusive sizing and style needs are welcome as inquiries, but do not assume a size or piece is currently in stock.
